# Product scope

LASTLIGHT helps energy consultants and facility teams explore how local backup systems behave when grid power is lost. The intended journey is a night district, a selected building, its energy equipment, an outage, a visible failure point, and a comparison of improvements.

The school is the primary demonstration site and now has a physics-calculated
outage trace, timeline and verified failure frontier. The clinic, small business
and residential sites remain synthetic visual archetypes. Inputs are synthetic;
there are no measured local building loads or surveyed utility topology. A
missing backend is explicitly labelled as a visual rehearsal with no calculated
figures.

The school demonstration uses one simulation result for every displayed energy
figure and physical lighting state. Runs identify inputs, units, assumptions,
versions and provenance. Optional FragilityNet proposals are checked by physics
and marked separately. An ML score never grants engineering clearance. A visual
change alone is never presented as a calculated survival result.

LASTLIGHT is a scenario analysis tool for early design. It does not operate a live grid or provide regulatory engineering signoff.
