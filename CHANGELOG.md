# Change log

## 24 October 2026

### Failure Frontier jury experience

Replaced the compact debug chart with a trace-derived school verdict and an
expandable three-chapter report. Added response-scaled axes, accessible cell
inspection, a distinct exact-run marker, discrete verified failure-time ticks,
real AI vs Physics analysis and a switchable verified-candidate overlay.
Preserved survival/recovery distinctions, model-unavailable fallback and existing
3D behavior. Added a guided walkthrough, responsive/keyboard regressions and
same-scenario browser evidence. Physics, ML weights and scene files are unchanged.

### Physics and ML foundation

Integrated the existing team physics API contribution, added variable load
profiles and generated wire contracts. Trained and evaluated FragilityNet with
an independent logistic baseline, equipment-group holdouts and checkpointed
preprocessing. Added physics-verified frontiers and measured sampling benchmarks;
the exact physical method remains the displayed reference. Prepared an actual
BDG2 research subset with evaluated cold-start and historical demand baselines.
Connected the school outage and lighting to one real trace, retaining explicit
rehearsal fallback. Extended API, browser and CI verification. Geometry, assets,
site positions and camera trajectories are preserved.

### First visual prototype

Built a React and TypeScript app with a synthetic district, facility selection, a systems layer and an assumptions drawer. The scene established the interaction flow but its flat composition did not meet the visual target.

### Cinematic district rebuild

Rebuilt the district as a persistent 3D world with directed camera movement. Added a detailed school, visible energy equipment, source inspection, a synthetic grid outage and reset. Captured the live browser route, screenshots at laptop and projector sizes, and direct WebGL keyframes.

### Public repository

Prepared a focused source history, product and engineering boundaries, browser evidence and automated build, lint and interaction checks. The validated energy simulation remains the next dependency.

### Simulation research reference

Preserved the earlier single-bus calculation and its 11 unit tests in a separate reference directory. The live scene still has no physical dispatch connection.
