# Audio and type provenance

- Sound bed and all impacts, pulses and whooshes were synthesized in `make_sound.py` for this film. No external recordings, music, voice or samples are used.
- The same original synthesis is delivered as `audio/original-full.wav` and `audio/original-speech-safe.wav`. The speech-safe version is 36% of the full amplitude before final AAC encoding.
- Manrope and IBM Plex Mono are installed from Fontsource. Both packages declare SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- No stock footage or generated replacement scenes are used. The district, school and blackout imagery come from the running LASTLIGHT WebGL application.

The render uses the full original mix. The speech and muted editions share the same H.264 picture and replace or omit the audio stream.
